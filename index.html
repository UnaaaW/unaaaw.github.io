ABOUT ME

Hi! I’m Yuyan (Una ()) Wu.

I’m currently a second-year ScM student in Computer Science at Brown University, where I'm advised by Prof. Daniel Ritchie and Prof. Adriana Schulz. Previously, I was a research intern at the University of Tokyo under the guidance of Prof. Takeo Igarashi. I received my B.A. in Information Science and Cognitive Science from Cornell University, where I had the pleasure of working with Prof. Donald P. Greenberg.

My research lies at the intersection of computer graphics, human–computer interaction, and computational design. I'm interested in developing interactive tools that make 3D design more intuitive, expressive, and accessible. In particular, I explore how computational systems can better bridge human design intent with geometric representations, modeling operations, and fabrication constraints.

I’m actively looking for PhD positions for Fall 2027!
